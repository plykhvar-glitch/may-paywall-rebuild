What this is

We went through May’s onboarding as an expecting parent, then rebuilt the screen where the trial is decided.

Adapty powers subscriptions for 30,000+ apps, so we see what moves conversion across the market, not inside one app. We installed May, answered every onboarding question, watched it calculate a due date, reached your paywall, closed it, played the gift game, read your App Store listings in Spain and France, and rebuilt those screens.

There is a lot to keep: a real 7-day trial, an honest yearly price, a beautiful gift game and a second offer when people close. But the paywall says almost nothing about May. In the version we saw, the top half of the screen is empty. The due date the onboarding just worked out, the expert answers, the 3D baby, your 4.8 rating and your 98% all appear before the paywall, and none of them appear on it.

We did not redesign your paywall

Your cream screen, the Lora headline with the lilac accent, the strikethrough and mint discount chips, “Seguro con App Store”, the white footer with “Elegido por 1560 personas hoy”, the dark green button, the gift game and the −74% card all stay. We fill the empty space with your own components.
